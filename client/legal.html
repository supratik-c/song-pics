Privacy Policy

Effective date: [Launch date — to be finalized before publishing]. This policy covers Scribble Bops (scribblebops.com) for players aged 16 and over in the UK, EU, and US.

Controller and contact. Scribble Bops is operated by [Operator name — to be finalized]. Contact: PLACEHOLDER@EMAIL.COM.

What the game itself does not collect. Scribble Bops has no player account, no backend database, no advertising tracker, no newsletter, and no contact form. The operator does not receive your guesses — puzzles are solved entirely in your browser.

Saved progress. Your normalized guesses and completion status for each puzzle are saved in your browser's localStorage, keyed by puzzle date. This stays on your device until you or your browser clears site data; the operator cannot remotely view, export, correct, or erase it.

Hosting. Scribble Bops is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare's edge network processes ordinary request data — IP address, request time, requested path, and user-agent — for delivery, security, and logging, under Cloudflare's own role and terms.

Analytics. The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to see aggregate visit counts, approximate visitor location (country), referrers, device type, and page-load performance. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other sites, and does not build an individual profile — see Cloudflare's Web Analytics policy for details.

YouTube. A solved or revealed puzzle offers a "Watch YouTube Video" control. Nothing is requested from YouTube until you activate it; from then on, later results in the same browser tab load their video automatically, and this choice is forgotten when the tab closes. Playback uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced player. Once loaded, YouTube may process your IP address, device information, cookies, and viewing activity under Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing a puzzle. The share button hands a prepared invitation and link to your device's own share sheet, or copies it to your clipboard if sharing isn't available. Scribble Bops does not learn what happens to it afterward.

Lawful bases. Saved progress and the YouTube session preference are stored only to provide the feature you asked for (strictly necessary storage). Hosting and security logging rely on the operator's legitimate interest in operating a working, secure site. No profiling or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects takes place, and no personal data is sold.

Retention. Local storage values persist only on your device, for as long as you keep them. Hosting and security logs are retained only as long as Cloudflare's own terms provide.

Your rights. Because saved progress and the YouTube preference live only in your browser, you can view, change, or erase them at any time using your browser's site-data controls — the operator has no separate copy to correct or delete. For hosting or analytics data, or any other privacy question, contact PLACEHOLDER@EMAIL.COM. UK and EU residents may also lodge a complaint with the ICO or their local supervisory authority.

Changes. If Scribble Bops adds a new feature that changes what is collected — for example, a support link — this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date above.
